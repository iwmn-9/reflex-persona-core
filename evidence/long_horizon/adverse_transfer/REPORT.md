The candidate changes the categorical world predictor used by both factual learning and hypothetical futures. The original half-prior on supplied labeled hypotheses stays unchanged. Within the unknown half, an equal mixture compares fresh Dirichlet laws with label permutations of supplied probability shapes. This proposes an unprovided tendency without knowing which response is actually favorable. It does not create actions or learn preferences. It uses no game/action-name branch, evaluator law or future real observation. Exact permutation completion is bounded to at most four response categories; larger alphabets and empty libraries retain the original predictor.

The structural assumption is that an unknown tendency may share a supplied probability shape. It can be wrong. Simple empirical reuse and wholesale label symmetrization were screened and rejected as default replacements. Pure symmetrization's fresh 128-stream result, including its loss of labeled priors, is retained in `../adverse_symmetric/prediction_screen/`. The final hierarchy's separately registered 128 streams / 16,384 observations are in `prediction_screen/`: nine of twelve changed alphabet/pattern groups improve mean expected log regret and Brier excess; the three- and four-category novel-shape groups and the three-category switching group worsen. All four five-category groups match the unchanged fallback exactly. Those scores are prediction evidence, not game-intelligence acceptance. The initial prose count of ten favorable groups omitted the switching loss; this was corrected against the unchanged complete data.

What this candidate may gain is learning an unprovided tendency from a reusable structure instead of starting every unknown law from an unstructured prior. It spends inference work and can lose when that structure is inappropriate. The long-play study, not this explanation, determines whether that trade is useful.

every paired action disagreement on detached copies. Each receives its actual
forecast, the other predictor's forecast, or the evaluator's true **current**
law. None of this privileged information enters factual learning. These are
local causal comparisons, not long-run optimal-policy evaluations.

One informative resource case (steady persona, seed 2026102941, outside)
improves an overconfident response estimate from 0.84145 to 0.80805 when the
true probability is 0.8. The new trajectory then trades for all 128 turns,
losing 34 realized currency versus the old predictor. At the first differing
decision, the unchanged personal Policy prefers the growth action even with
the true law; the additional 0.05 reader-adoption margin vetoes it. Thus this
loss cannot simply be described as worse prediction or an intentional
personality sacrifice. The shared selection interface is a separate research
target. Tests of that interface must retain Policy risk, principles, intent,
progress masks and the validation boundary for genuinely competing advice.
